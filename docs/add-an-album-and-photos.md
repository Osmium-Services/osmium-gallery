**Osmium Gallery** keeps your photos in albums. Your site's design decides where and how the albums are shown.

## Create an album

1. Open **Osmium Gallery** and press **Add Album**.
2. Enter a **Name**. Add an **Event date** and a **Description** if you want them (both are optional).
3. Turn on **Published** only if the album should go on the website straight away. Leave it off to keep it as a draft.
4. Press **Save album**.

The album's web address is made from its name when you create it and does not change if you rename the album later.

## Add photos

A new album has no photos until it is saved. After saving you land on the album's page.

1. Drag photos onto the box, or click it to choose them. JPG, PNG and WebP are accepted, up to the size shown in the box.
2. Each photo is uploaded on its own. If one is refused, its name is listed with the reason and the rest carry on.

## Order, cover and captions

- **Order:** drag the rows in the **Photos** list. They are shown on the site in that order.
- **Cover:** the cover is the picture that stands for the album in lists. Use **Make cover** on a photo. With no cover chosen, the first photo is used.
- **Caption and alt text:** use **Edit caption and alt**. The caption is shown with the photo. The alt text describes the photo for people who cannot see it, and the caption is used if you leave it empty.
- **Rotate:** use **Edit photo** to open the photo, turn it left or right, then press save.

## Publish, hide or delete

- In the **Albums** list, use **Publish** or **Unpublish**. A draft album is not shown on the site.
- **Delete** removes an album from the list. You can bring it back from **Deleted Albums**.
- Deleting a photo works the same way: restore it from **Deleted Photos**. **Permanently Delete** removes the photo and its original for good, and only an administrator sees it.
